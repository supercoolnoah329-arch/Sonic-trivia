# Original Mascot Assets

`original-raccoon.png` and `original-score-raider.png` are original project artwork generated locally for this fan-made trivia game. They are intentionally not depictions of Sonic, Tails, Eggman, or any official SEGA character.

- `original-raccoon.png`: green raccoon mechanic used for correct-answer feedback.
- `original-score-raider.png`: red-and-gold egg-shaped score raider used for wrong-answer feedback.

The files have transparent backgrounds and may be modified as part of this project.
